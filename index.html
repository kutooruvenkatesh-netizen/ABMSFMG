About the company

Fast, reliable facilities management, built around your building's real demands.

ABMS Facilities Management Services L.L.C. S.P.C. provides professional facilities management services across a wide range of sectors — IT staffing, civil works, MEP, commercial and deep cleaning, gardening, pest control and car cleaning.

We're committed to meeting the changing demands of our clients with fast, efficient, and reliable service, aiming for high quality and complete satisfaction on every job.

Our Vision

To address the evolving demands of the cleaning and facilities industry by leveraging cutting-edge technology — staying forward-thinking and leading through exceptional service and client satisfaction.

Our Mission

To be customer-centric, delivering reliable, high-quality services tailored to each client. We invest continuously in our team's training and in state-of-the-art equipment to meet every need effectively.

  • Foster a culture of excellence and integrity in all our operations
  • Implement environmentally sustainable practices to minimize our ecological footprint
  • Build long-lasting relationships with clients by understanding and anticipating their needs
  • Innovate our services regularly to stay ahead in an evolving industry
